# User research: where newcomers lose money

Desk research, November 2026. Goal: find the money problems immigrants, international students and first-time filers hit most in the U.S., and decide what Pikalo builds first.

## Findings

| #   | Problem                                                               | Who                               | Why it costs money                                                                                                                                                             | Source                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | FICA (Social Security + Medicare) withheld when it shouldn't be       | F-1/J-1/M-1 nonresident students  | Payroll withholds 7.65% of wages the student doesn't owe. Refund needs the employer, then IRS Forms 843 + 8316.                                                                | [IRS: Foreign student liability for Social Security and Medicare taxes](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/foreign-student-liability-for-social-security-and-medicare-taxes)                                                                                                               |
| 2   | Filing a resident return (1040, mainstream software) as a nonresident | International students            | Wrong credits claimed, amended returns, possible effect on future visa applications. Common enough that schools publish "misfiled tax help" pages.                             | [Minnesota State Mankato: Misfiled tax help](https://mankato.mnsu.edu/academics/global-education/centers-of-global-education/international-student-services/rules-and-regulations/taxes/misfiled-tax-help/); [TrackMyOPT: filing mistakes](https://www.trackmyopt.com/blog/f1-opt-stem-opt-tax-filing-mistakes) |
| 3   | Skipping Form 8843                                                    | Exempt individuals with no income | Required even with zero income; often missed.                                                                                                                                  | Same as above                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 4   | No U.S. credit history ("credit invisible")                           | All newcomers                     | Larger deposits, rejected rentals, higher insurance. In a 300-person survey, 49% said a credit card was the hardest product to get. Survey sponsored by a credit-data company. | [Nova Credit survey](https://novacredit.com/blog/the-impact-of-financial-services-access-on-immigrants); [WAMC](https://www.wamc.org/2023-05-24/welcome-to-america-now-learn-to-be-in-debt)                                                                                                                     |
| 5   | Ghost preparers and notario fraud                                     | Immigrant communities             | Preparers who won't sign, take cash, charge a % of the refund, or route refunds to their own account. Notarios giving illegal immigration advice.                              | [GAO: ghost preparers](https://www.gao.gov/blog/beware-ghost-preparers-they-can-haunt-your-tax-filings); [Connecticut: scams targeting immigrants](https://portal.ct.gov/SmartConsumer/SmartConsumer/Immigrants-and-Refugees/Common-Scams-Targeting-Immigrants---Do)                                            |
| 6   | Unreported home-country bank accounts (FBAR)                          | New tax residents                 | U.S. persons with foreign accounts totaling over $10,000 at any time in the year must file FinCEN 114; penalties are large.                                                    | [IRS: FBAR](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/report-of-foreign-bank-and-financial-accounts-fbar)                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 7   | Roth IRA eligibility on a visa                                        | OPT, H-1B                         | Confusion over who can contribute; amounts excluded from income (e.g. treaty-exempt wages) aren't compensation.                                                                | [IRS Pub 590-A](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p590a); [IRS: 2026 limits](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/401k-limit-increases-to-24500-for-2026-ira-limit-increases-to-7500)                                                                                                                                    |
| 8   | Remittance fees                                                       | Anyone sending money home         | Fees plus exchange-rate markup; the UN target is under 3% per transfer.                                                                                                        | [World Bank SDG 10.c.1](https://worldbank.github.io/sdg-metadata/phase-2/metadata/en/10-c-1)                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 9   | Missed tax treaty benefits                                            | Students from treaty countries    | Benefits must be claimed and disclosed (Form 8833, $1,000 penalty for failing to file it when required).                                                                       | [IRS Pub 901](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p901)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |

## What we build first

1. **FICA refund checker:** turns problem 1 into a dollar amount and a step list.
2. **Roth IRA calculator:** 2026 limits and phase-outs, with the visa-specific compensation rule.
3. **Money traps guide:** problems 2–9 as short, sourced explainers with "what to do."
4. **Home page by situation:** route people to the tools that apply to them.

## Rule for every number

Numbers come from tested functions in `lib/` with an IRS or government source in the code, never hand-typed into page copy.
